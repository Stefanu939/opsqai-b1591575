# LinkedIn launch post for OPSQAI (English) + Pilot offer

Goal: one pain-first English post published to your LinkedIn profile, with 2-3 images, a link to opsqai.de, and a Pilot programme offer of up to 50% off.

## What I will write

Three complete drafts, all pain-first, so you can pick the one that sounds most like you:

**Option A - "The auditor question"**
Opens with the moment an auditor asks "how do you know the current procedure was followed?" and nobody can answer without a scavenger hunt. Then: where that pain comes from (knowledge in shared drives, wikis and people's heads), why public AI tools are off the table (the data can never leave the company), and how OPSQAI answers it - Windows self-hosted, local AI, source-grounded answers with citations, refusal when no source exists, audit trail, licensed products for Operations, Quality & Compliance, Logistics, Transport, HR, Finance and Inventory.

**Option B - "Six months to become useful"**
Opens with new joiners taking months to be productive while the same twenty questions get asked over and over. Same middle, but the payoff leans on Academy, SOPs and FAQ deflection.

**Option C - "We can't send that to ChatGPT"**
Opens with the sentence every compliance officer says. Payoff leans on sovereignty: your server, your data, your model, nothing leaving the building.

Each draft follows what actually performs on LinkedIn right now:
- a 1-2 line hook that lands above the "see more" fold, no link in the first lines
- short single-thought lines, no wall of text, no engagement-bait questions
- 900-1,300 characters of body, concrete specifics instead of adjectives
- the site link placed in the post body near the end (and repeated in the first comment)
- 3-5 relevant hashtags at the very bottom
- a clear single call to action: Pilot programme

No fake urgency, no invented certifications. You approve the exact wording before publishing.
